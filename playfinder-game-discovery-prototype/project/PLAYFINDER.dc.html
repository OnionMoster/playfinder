How it works

  1. Tell us your taste, budget and platform once.
  2. We surface six games composed around those preferences.
  3. Games reviewed or interacted with during the training period are excluded; this is not ownership data.
  4. Genre filters match only explicit source genres. Tags may support taste matching, but are never treated as genres.
  5. Refine with the editorial filters — genres, tags, price and platform combine.
  6. Like, dislike, wishlist or dismiss any game to shape the set.
  7. Everything stays anonymous and on this device.
