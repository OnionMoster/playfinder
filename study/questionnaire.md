# PlayFinder questionnaire

Use a 1–7 response scale after each of the three rounds:

1. Strongly disagree
2. Disagree
3. Somewhat disagree
4. Neither agree nor disagree
5. Somewhat agree
6. Agree
7. Strongly agree

## Ten per-round Likert items

1. The recommended games matched my interests.
2. The list contained games I would genuinely consider playing.
3. The recommendations included useful variety rather than feeling repetitive.
4. The list helped me discover games I did not already know well.
5. I felt confident choosing a game from this list.
6. The recommendations fit my budget after I used the filters.
7. The recommendations fit the platform or platforms I selected.
8. It was easy to narrow the list to suitable games.
9. I could understand why these games might have been shown to me.
10. Overall, I was satisfied with this recommendation round.

Record every answer even when the participant rejected all games. Do not add
the item scores into one total until internal consistency and missingness have
been inspected.

## Two final open questions

1. What most influenced which games you accepted or rejected?
2. Which of the three rounds did you prefer, why, and what would you improve?

## Planned constructs

- perceived relevance: Q1–Q2;
- diversity and discovery: Q3–Q4;
- decision confidence: Q5;
- constraint fit: Q6–Q7;
- usability and transparency: Q8–Q9;
- overall satisfaction: Q10.

Q10 is the primary single-item online satisfaction outcome. Construct averages
are secondary and should be reported with their item definitions.
